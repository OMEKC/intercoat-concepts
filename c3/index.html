04Technical service

From technical audit
to routine production.

We are responsible for the final result.

The aim is to select a coating and define test conditions that reflect the actual product and coating line, rather than choosing a powder based on colour reference alone.

The delivery includes a visit for a technical specialist.

The technical audit can cover the substrate, surface preparation, part shape, application conditions, film build, curing conditions, current defects, process limitations and acceptance criteria. Relevant technical documentation can also be reviewed before production approval, including TDS, SDS, certificates and test protocols where available.

01

Finish & texture
Innovation basis

Intercoat develops powder coatings using advances in synthetic resin and pigment technology, together with optimised particle-size distribution.

Particle-size distribution influences powder fluidisation, electrostatic charging, powder transfer, penetration into difficult-to-coat areas, deposition on the part and powder recovery.

06Think Nature

Powder coatings recycling.

Intercoat accepts powder coatings from customers for recovery or recycling.

This can include fine powder, overspray, cyclone-recovered powder, obsolete batches, slow-moving colours and surplus stock.

Fine powderOversprayCyclone-recovered powderObsolete batchesSlow-moving coloursSurplus stock

The collected material is sorted and, where technically possible, directed to recovery or responsible recycling. This helps customers reduce powder coatings and make better use of material that would otherwise require disposal.
