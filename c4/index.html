04Technical service

From technical audit
to routine production.

We are responsible for the final result.

The delivery includes a visit for a technical specialist.

The aim is to select a coating and define test conditions that reflect the actual product and coating line, rather than choosing a powder based on colour reference alone.

01

Technical audit

A technical specialist reviews the product, the coating line and the current process, so that requirements are defined before any coating is chosen.

02

Coating selection

Choose the series, colour, texture and required properties for the product and the coating line.

03

Testing

Relevant technical documentation can be reviewed before production approval, including TDS, SDS, certificates and test protocols where available.

04

Production trial

Discuss line conditions, typical defects and requirements before a large production trial.

05

Routine production use

The outcome: it is clear which series to use, which documents are needed and what to check on the line.

Discuss a custom formulation
Formulation
Finish & texture
Innovation basis

Intercoat develops powder coatings using advances in synthetic resin and pigment technology, together with optimised particle-size distribution.

Particle-size distribution influences powder fluidisation, electrostatic charging, powder transfer, penetration into difficult-to-coat areas, deposition on the part and powder recovery.
