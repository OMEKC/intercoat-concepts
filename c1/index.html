04We are responsible for the final result

From technical audit
to routine production

Changing your coating supplier should not start with guesswork.

Every delivery includes a visit from a technical specialist.

The technical audit can cover the substrate, surface preparation, part shape, application conditions, film build, curing conditions, current defects, process limitations and acceptance criteria. Relevant documentation — TDS, SDS, certificates and test protocols — can be reviewed before production approval.

05Custom solutions

When a standard
coating is not enough

Intercoat develops coatings using advances in synthetic resin and pigment technology, together with optimised particle-size distribution.

Formulations adjusted to your requirements

Intercoat develops and adjusts coating formulations for specific customer requirements, including colour and decorative effects, application behaviour, curing conditions and other functional properties.

Depending on the task, development can progress from laboratory samples to testing and a production trial on the customer’s coating line.

Particle size

Particle-size distribution influences fluidisation, electrostatic charging, powder transfer, penetration into difficult-to-coat areas, deposition on the part and powder recovery.

06Think Nature

Powder coatings recycling

Powder that never reaches the part doesn’t have to become waste.

Intercoat accepts powder coatings from customers for recovery or recycling. The collected material is sorted and, where technically possible, directed to recovery or responsible recycling.
