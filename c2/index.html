Changing your coating supplier should not start with guesswork.

Support from lab to line

Every delivery includes a visit from a technical specialist.

01

Technical audit

The audit can cover the substrate, surface preparation, part shape, application conditions, film build, curing conditions, current defects, process limitations and acceptance criteria.

02

Coating selection

Choose the series, colour, texture and required properties for the product and the coating line.

03

TDS, SDS, certificates and test protocols can be reviewed before production approval.

04
